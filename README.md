## The entry

> A queue is a caterpillar made of people, each person a little segment shuffling forward one wiggle at a time until the head reaches the counter.
>
> The caterpillar fits because a queue really does creep forward in small steps from the back to the front, but it leaves out that people can step out, jump in, or grumble, which no caterpillar segment ever does.
